Got Questions?
Frequently Asked

What areas do you serve?

We provide delivery and pickup service within the following cities: Longview, Hallsville, Kilgore, White Oak, and Gladewater. If your address falls within any of these cities, you're in our service area. Need service outside these cities? Contact us — we may be able to accommodate for an additional delivery fee.

How big are the totes?

Our totes are 27-gallon heavy-duty storage containers measuring 30.6" W × 20.6" D × 14.3" H. Each tote holds up to 100 lbs. They're sturdy, lidded, and stack cleanly in any moving truck or trailer — roughly equivalent to 1.5 medium moving boxes in capacity.

What if I need them longer?

Extensions are $10.00 per day per 20 totes and can be arranged before your pickup date. Just reach out and we'll work with your timeline.

What is the cancellation policy?

You may cancel your order at no charge up to 48 hours before your scheduled delivery date. Cancellations made with less than 48 hours notice are subject to a $25.00 convenience fee.

Do you deliver inside my home or apartment?

No — Piney Woods Rentals is a door-to-door delivery service. We drop off and pick up at the nearest accessible point outside your home or apartment, typically your driveway, garage, or unit entrance. We do not carry items inside your home, apartment, or up stairs.

What happens if a tote is damaged?

Normal wear is no problem. Damaged or missing totes are billed at $15.00 per tote, flat dollies at $50.00 each, and the 2-wheel hand truck at $75.00 if applicable.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend 3–5 days' notice to guarantee your preferred date and delivery window, especially on weekends and end-of-month dates when demand is highest.

Do I need to be present at drop off / pick up?

Yes — someone 18 years of age or older must be present at the delivery address to accept the drop off and at the pickup location to release the equipment. This ensures we can verify the tote count and condition at both ends.

Do I need to clean the totes before return?

Just empty them out — we handle sanitizing between every rental. No wiping, no washing required on your end.
